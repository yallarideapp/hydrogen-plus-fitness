Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

This website presents Hydrogen Plus Fitness, Al-Husn Street, opposite Granada College, Irbid, Jordan. For privacy questions, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, call +962 7 9215 1000 or contact the gym through its official Instagram or Facebook page.

Information you choose to share

An enquiry collects your name, phone number, optional email, selected service, optional preferred date, message, and consent. Signed-in visitors may save workout names, dates, duration, notes and optional weight measurements. Please do not submit medical records or other sensitive details in free-text fields. Weight measurements are optional and are processed only when you expressly consent.

Accounts and purpose

Sign in with ChatGPT provides a site-specific user identifier, email and, when available, display name. These are used to show your own requests and fitness records. Staff email addresses explicitly approved by the site owner can access enquiry details in the staff inbox and update their status. They do not receive access to your workout or weight records. Enquiries are saved as pending requests; they do not automatically reserve a place or notify the gym through email or SMS. For an immediate response, call the gym.

Storage and service providers

Records are stored using Cloudflare D1 through OpenAI Sites. OpenAI supplies site hosting and sign-in services, and Cloudflare supplies infrastructure. Our application does not intentionally store your IP address. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical connection logs under their own policies; their retention periods have not been verified by the gym.

Your browser and external links

The hp-language localStorage entry remembers your language until you clear browser storage. The site loads its fonts and photos from its own origin and does not install advertising pixels or analytics. Authentication uses platform-managed cookies. Clicking external social or Google Maps links opens those providers and subjects your visit to their policies.

Retention and your choices

You can delete signed-in fitness data and linked requests from the member area. Anonymous enquiries cannot be linked to that account automatically; ask the gym to locate them using your request reference. The gym must confirm its operational retention schedule and backup deletion practices before general public launch. Consent withdrawal applies to future processing and does not undo processing already lawfully completed.

Jordanian law and complaints

Jordan’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023 applies where relevant. Raise a complaint with the gym first. If unresolved, you may escalate it to the Personal Data Protection Directorate at the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship using the Ministry’s official Services page.
