ABOUT

The north’s biggest training floor.

Men and women each have their own gym hours, and the club’s trainers are on the floor to help you set up machines and plan your sessions. Equipment spotlights on Instagram cover the vertical leg press, V-squat, Super Rowing machine, multi-rotary arm curl and dedicated back and upper-chest stations.

Can I see the gym before joining?

Yes. Send a visit request with your preferred day and the team will call you to arrange it.

Do men and women train at the same time?

The club publishes separate men’s and ladies’ hours. Ask the team which areas each session uses.

How much is a membership?

Prices change with each offer. The most recent offers are listed on News & offers — call the club for today’s price.
