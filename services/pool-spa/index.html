ROOFTOP POOL

Pool & spa

Train, then recharge by the water.

An outdoor rooftop pool with sun loungers, umbrellas and city views, plus spa sessions for post-workout recovery. The pool opens for the summer season with set ladies’ and men’s times.

QUESTIONS

Good to know

More answers in Help
Is the pool open all year?

It is an outdoor pool that has opened each summer (late May 2025, June 2026). Ask the team whether it is open on the day you plan to visit.

Can I use the pool without a gym membership?

Yes — the club has published a day price for non-members. Call ahead to confirm today’s price and session times.
