ABOUT

Irbid’s first reformer Pilates room.

The club’s Pilates highlight describes the room as designed for calm, energy and a safe space to move freely. Prenatal sessions focus on pelvic-floor and back strength, breathing and relaxation and easing lower-back discomfort — always check with your doctor first. In spring 2026 the studio took Pilates outdoors to the hills of Salt.
