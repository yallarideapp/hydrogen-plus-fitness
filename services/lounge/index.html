ABOUT

Big matches, slow evenings.

“More than a lounge — an experience,” as its own Instagram puts it. Expect specialty coffee and desserts like cheesecake, milkshakes, fresh lemon & mint and handcrafted mocktails, served poolside with the city lights below. Birthdays are welcome — the lounge has posted its own “make a wish” celebrations.

The lounge has screened the 2026 World Cup, El Clásico and Champions League nights, and hosts Ladies Night dinners. A new venue for special occasions, Hydrogen Garden, has been announced as opening soon.

QUESTIONS

Good to know

More answers in Help
Do I need to book for match nights?

Big matches fill up — the club has asked guests to reserve their seats. Call ahead.

Is the lounge only for members?

No. The club says the lounge is open to everyone.

Can I celebrate a birthday there?

Yes — the lounge has posted birthday celebrations. Call 079 837 0157 to arrange a table and a cake.
