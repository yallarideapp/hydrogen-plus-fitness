QUESTIONS

Good to know

More answers in Help
Are the classes on the timetable for women?

Yes — the weekly timetable the club publishes is the women’s class schedule.

Are the coaches women?

The women’s classes are led by the coaches named on the timetable, and the women’s swimming course is supervised by female coaches.

Is the pool private for women?

The club publishes dedicated ladies’ pool hours each season. Check the latest hours with the team before you visit.
